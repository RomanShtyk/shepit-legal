Shepit Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This policy explains how the Shepit voice recorder app ("the App"), developed by Roman Shtykalo ("I", "me"), handles your information. I am the data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Your recordings stay on your device

Audio you record or import, transcripts, translations, bookmarks, tags and settings are stored only on your device. Transcription runs locally with whisper.cpp and translation runs locally (ML Kit on Android, Apple Translation on iOS). The App has no account and no server of mine. I never receive your audio or text.

2. Model downloads

Premium transcription models and translation language packs are downloaded from GitHub, Hugging Face, Google or Apple when you choose them. Only an ordinary file request is sent; it contains no content from the App.

3. Purchases (RevenueCat)

Premium is sold through the App Store or Google Play. The App sends the store receipt and an anonymous app user ID to RevenueCat to check what you own. I do not receive your name, email or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows one banner ad on the library screen. Google AdMob may process your advertising identifier, IP address and device information to serve and measure ads. On iOS, personalised ads are used only if you allow tracking when asked; in the EU/EEA and UK you are asked for consent first. Premium removes ads. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from partners.

5. Crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics)

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect and fix crashes. A crash report contains a stack trace, app state, device and app information (model, OS version, app version, free memory) and a Crashlytics installation identifier. Crash reports never include your recordings or transcripts. They are retained for 90 days. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

6. Permissions

  • Microphone — to record. Used only while you are recording.
  • Notifications — to show recording and transcription progress.
  • Tracking (iOS) — optional, asked only for ad personalisation. Declining changes nothing except ad relevance.

7. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Contract — validating purchases to provide Premium.
  • Legitimate interest — crash reporting and non-personalised ads in the free version.
  • Consent — personalised ads and tracking.

8. International transfers

Google (AdMob, Firebase) and RevenueCat may process data outside the EU/EEA, including in the United States, under Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

9. Retention and deletion

Your recordings and transcripts are kept on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall it. Nothing is retained by me because nothing is collected by me. Crash reports expire after 90 days; ad and purchase data is governed by Google's and RevenueCat's own retention policies.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You can withdraw ad consent in the App's settings or your device's privacy settings. For anything else, contact me at the address below. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU) and does not knowingly collect data from them.

12. Changes

I may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
